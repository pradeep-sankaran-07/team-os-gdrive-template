# Logo

**This template ships no logo file on purpose.** A public template cannot carry
a real company's mark, and a placeholder logo has a way of surviving into a
customer deliverable.

During install, drop your primary and reversed logo files into
`.claude/skills/acme-brand/assets/` and name them `logo.svg` and
`logo-reversed.svg`. Until they exist, this skill asks for the logo rather than
producing an unbranded document.

Clear space: at least the height of the logo on every side. Minimum width: 90px
on screen, 24mm in print. Never recolour it, never stretch it, never put the
primary version on a dark background.
