## Capabilities and constraints

Contact and company enrichment, which is what sits behind Acme Outbound's
sourcing module.

Constraints worth knowing: coverage in Germany and the Nordics is materially
thinner than in the US and UK, and they do not guarantee refresh frequency on
smaller companies. Both of these show up as customer-visible quality, which is
why they are written here rather than assumed.

## Commercial (this folder only)

Volume-tiered, billed monthly on records enriched. Annual commitment with a
quarterly true-up. Acme margin and customer pricing are not recorded here.

## Customers served

Indirectly, all of them. Coverage gaps are most visible at
[ashgrove-analytics](../../accounts/ashgrove-analytics/), which sells into Germany.

## Notes

The coverage gap is the single most likely source of a "the data is wrong"
complaint from a customer, and it is a known limitation rather than a defect.
