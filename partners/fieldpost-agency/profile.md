## Capabilities and constraints

A demand-generation agency that resells Acme Outbound to its own clients and
runs the sequences on their behalf.

Constraint: their clients are smaller than our direct ICP, typically under 50
employees, which means they hit the thin end of the enrichment coverage more
often than our direct customers do.

## Commercial (this folder only)

Reseller margin per seat, invoiced monthly. Acme's own pricing to direct
customers does not appear here and must not be shared with them.

## Customers served

Their end clients are not Acme accounts and do not get folders under
`accounts/`. Fieldpost is the customer of record.

## Notes

Useful as an early warning system: because their clients are smaller, they hit
data-quality problems before our direct customers do.
