# Acme Corp

**Demo content.** Acme Corp is fictional. Replace this folder with your own
company during install; the file names and the questions they answer are the
part that transfers.

## What we do

Acme Corp sells **Acme Outbound**, software that runs outbound sales for
mid-market B2B companies: it finds the right accounts, runs the sequences,
handles the replies, and keeps the sending domains healthy.

## Business model

Per-seat subscription, priced per AI rep, plus usage for enrichment and sending
volume. Annual contracts, quarterly in advance. Implementation is included and
is deliberately short, which is a positioning choice as much as a pricing one.

## Where we are

Roughly two years old, with six people using this Team OS and five customer
records in it. A real company of this size would have more of both; the seeded
set is small enough to read end to end. Customers are mid-market
B2B software and services companies in North America and Europe, typically with
a sales team of 5 to 40 and an existing CRM they will not replace.

## The problem we actually solve

Outbound at volume is easy to start and hard to keep alive. The failure is
rarely sending; it is what happens afterwards. Replies pile up unclassified,
follow-up is inconsistent, domains get flagged, and within a quarter the team
has quietly stopped trusting the tool. Acme Outbound is built around the part
after the send.

## Constraints worth knowing

- We do not replace the CRM. Every deal assumes one is already in place.
- We do not sell to companies without an existing sales motion. There has to be
  something to scale.
- Implementation is short by design. A deal that needs a long implementation is
  a deal we have mispriced or misqualified.
